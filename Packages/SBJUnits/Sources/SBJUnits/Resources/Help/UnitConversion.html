TITLE

Convert values between compatible units while preserving the represented physical quantity.

Converting

  • Enter the source value and choose its unit.
  • Choose the destination unit to see the converted result.
  • UI_swapUnits\ Swap source and destination units. The current result becomes the new source value.
  • UI_resetValue\ Reset the source amount to one.
  • UI_selected\ A checkmark identifies the currently selected unit in a unit menu.

When step controls are available, their increments depend on the selected unit and its settings.
